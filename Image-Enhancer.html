Hardware Requirements for Real-Time Enhancement

Fine-grained adjustments on high-resolution images are processed per pixel on the CPU. For smooth, real-time slider feedback we recommend at least 4 GB of free RAM, a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox), and a device from the last 4–5 years. Older or low-RAM devices may experience a slight delay while dragging sliders on very large photos.
